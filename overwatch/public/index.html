BEFORE YOUR FIRST SCAN

Set up your kitchen pilot

  1. Print the label pack. Attach product labels to rice, oats, tea, and a can. Equivalent props are fine if you label them accordingly.
  2. Place the five checkpoint labels. One corridor: Receiving → West → Rack A → East → Rack B. West and East are label IDs, not compass bearings. Place them in this order along the accessible passage.
  3. Label four shelf positions. A-R1-S1 and A-R1-S2 in a dry storage zone near the kitchen entrance; B-R1-S1 and B-R1-S2 in a second dry zone farther along the passage. Use accessible shelves or marked trays; keep away from the sink and cooker.
  4. Match the map to your room. The printed setup page shows a single corridor with no assumed alternate route. If your kitchen differs, edit warehouse.json edges on your laptop before starting.
  5. Enable audio. Connect your headphones through Android, press Voice on, then Repeat instruction. A mic command requires its own permission.
  6. Scan a batch, send for laptop review, then begin put-away. Open Laptop review on the laptop, review quantities, and approve the batch. On the phone select an approved item and scan RECEIVING. At the destination scan the shelf, rescan the product, physically place it, and confirm.
